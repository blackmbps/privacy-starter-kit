# The 8-question decision tree (your laws, in 3 minutes)

Answer yes/no/unsure. Your "yes" answers are your sector overlay; feed them
to Prompt 3.

1. Any health or wellness data (mood, sleep, symptoms, fitness)?
   → FTC Health Breach Notification Rule; WA My Health My Data; HIPAA only if
   you serve covered entities (plans, providers).
2. Any financial data (accounts, credit, lending)?
   → GLBA if you're a financial institution; FCRA if used for eligibility.
3. Any users under 13 (or do you know some are)?
   → COPPA.
4. Users in CA, CO, VA, CT, UT or the EU?
   → state comprehensive laws (CCPA/CPRA etc.); GDPR for the EU.
5. Sell or share data with ad tech?
   → CCPA/CPRA "sale/share" opt-outs; state universal opt-out signals.
6. What state do you do business out of?
   → your home state's comprehensive law and breach-notification statute.
7. Any education data (students, schools)?
   → FERPA if school-funded records; state student-privacy laws.
8. Any biometric or precise location data?
   → Illinois BIPA; biometric/location provisions in state laws.

HIPAA almost never applies to consumer wellness apps. That is NOT the same
as unregulated: questions 1 and 4-6 usually apply instead.
